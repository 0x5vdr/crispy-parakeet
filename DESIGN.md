## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated precision, clarity, and psychological calm required by high-performing quantitative traders and prop-firm participants. Departing from legacy financial terminals, brutalist execution screens, or neon-drenched crypto dashboards, this system crafts a sanctuary of disciplined focus. It positions performance tracking not as chaotic trading, but as a deliberate craft rooted in clarity, edge verification, and routine.

### Design Movement: Modern High-Caliber SaaS Fintech
The aesthetic sits at the intersection of modern deep-surface minimalism and tactile software craftsmanship:
- **Calm, Low-Strain Surfaces**: Layered deep navy-charcoal canvases (`#0D0F17` transitioning into `#161822` cards) eliminate high-contrast harshness and terminal glare while retaining absolute clarity during lengthy analysis sessions.
- **Architectural Softness**: Fluid contours (12px–16px radii on major spatial nodes and 999px pill containers) offset the mathematical rigidity of PnL ledgers, replacing cold spreadsheets with a warm, consumer-grade enterprise experience.
- **Functional Semantics**: Vivid emerald greens and rose reds are deployed strictly as purposeful semantic data markers rather than ambient decoration, ensuring cognitive speed and immediate signal recognition.

## Colors

The color palette is built for long-duration cognitive comfort with an intentional hierarchy. It relies on a dark navy/charcoal foundation that feels organic rather than purely synthetic.

### Application Rules
- **Canvas & Elevation**: The root view sits on `surface-canvas` (`#0D0F17`). Data nodes, stat modules, and grids are elevated via `surface-card` (`#161822`) with hairline outlines (`border-subtle` `#232736`). Never use pure `#000000` or neutral grey; retain the subtle navy undertone.
- **Data Signaling (Profit vs. Loss)**:
  - `gain-emerald` (`#10B981`) denotes positive expectancy, winning trades, and long positioning. Pair with `gain-emerald-subtle` backgrounds for badge chips and arrow indicators.
  - `loss-rose` (`#EF4444`) denotes drawdowns, losses, and short positioning. Pair with `loss-rose-subtle` backgrounds for alerts and short trade pills.
- **Brand Primary Accent**:
  - `accent-indigo` (`#6366F1`) drives non-directional primary operations (e.g., "+ Add Trade", active primary filters, primary UI triggers) preventing directional green/red confusion with systemic interactive states.
- **Text & Visual Hierarchy**:
  - `text-primary` (`#F8FAFC`): Bold numbers, major headline figures, high-priority trade identifiers.
  - `text-secondary` (`#94A3B8`): Standard table values, active filter states, navigation links.
  - `text-tertiary` (`#64748B`): Metric titles, tooltip trigger cues, commission details, column metadata headers.

## Typography

The typography is built around clean modern sans-serif geometry with zero reliance on monospace or terminal typefaces. The goal is a confident, top-tier SaaS aesthetic that organizes complex tabular data cleanly.

### Typographic Rules & Numerical Handling
- **Tabular Figures**: Every numeric representation (PnL, tick points, win rates, execution timestamps, lots) must explicitly inherit `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` or `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`. This provides vertical column alignment without introducing terminal aesthetic compromises.
- **Visual Weight**: Primary card figures lean into `stat-display` (28px Semibold/Bold). Subdued context tags above stat displays use `label-caps` in uppercase with letter spacing (`0.06em`) to anchor the visual scan.
- **Hierarchy Integrity**: The visual anchor of the data table is the `Net PnL` figure rendered in `data-mono-lookalike` (`14px Bold`) using semantic gain/loss colors.

## Layout & Spacing

The dashboard employs a fluid-constrained 12-column grid system tuned for 1440px viewports, expanding smoothly up to 1600px with generous margins. 

### Rhythmic Structure
- **Base Grid**: Built strictly on an 8px base rhythm (with 4px micro-steps for icons, badge insets, and inner chip padding).
- **Macro Layout**:
  - Vertical spacing between distinct sections (Top Bar to Key Metrics, Key Metrics to Equity Curve, Chart to Filter Bar, Filter Bar to Table) uses `space-2xl` (32px) to prevent cognitive density.
  - Page side gutters: `space-2xl` (32px) on desktop, transitioning to `space-md` (16px) on mobile.
- **Metrics Grid**: Full-width container parsed into 5 identical metric cards (`flex: 1` or `grid-cols-5`) separated by a `1rem` (16px) gap. On viewports below 1100px, reflow to 3 columns, then 2 columns on tablet, and single-column on mobile.
- **Table Density**: Table rows utilize `table-row-h` (52px–56px) with 16px horizontal internal padding. This ensures entries breathe naturally rather than collapsing into raw ledger output.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive skeuomorphism, extreme multi-layered drop shadows, or harsh borders. It crafts depth via tonal surfacing, low-contrast ghost strokes, and soft atmospheric inner glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (App Shell)**: `#0D0F17` (Deepest canvas layer).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: `#161822` with a uniform 1px perimeter border of `#232736`. This provides structural isolation from the canvas.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cells)**: Cards elevate to `#1C1F2E` with a border transition to `#32374E` accompanied by a soft, diffuse ambient shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Tooltips & Popovers)**: `#202436` container with `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` and a delicate interior top-border highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Chart Area Ambient Depth**: The primary equity chart employs an area gradient starting at `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.22)` at the peak of the curve, smoothly feathering into `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.00)` at the lower baseline.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary prioritizes softness and friendliness without compromising quantitative accuracy.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Parent Modules & Data Cards**: Configured with `rounded-xl` (14px–16px) to eliminate harsh rectangular angles while keeping viewport edge space efficient.
- **Buttons & Control Inputs**: Standard action buttons, date range pickers, and filter dropdowns leverage `0.625rem` (10px) to `0.75rem` (12px) rounding.
- **Status Badges, Indicators & Pills**: Directional tags (Buy/Sell, Long/Short), market sessions, and trade setups are strictly full pill structures: `9999px` radius (`rounded-full`).
- **Icon Accent Tiles**: Square performance trend indicators (holding arrow iconography) use `rounded-md` (6px–8px) on a 28px × 28px bounding box.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("+ Add Trade")**:
  - Background: `accent-indigo` (`#6366F1`)
  - Typography: `body-md-medium`, `#FFFFFF`
  - Inset Padding: 10px 20px
  - Corner Radius: 10px
  - Hover state: Brightens to `#4F46E5` with subtle button glow (`0 0 16px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Filter Controls**:
  - Background: Transparent or `#161822`
  - Border: 1px solid `#232736`
  - Corner Radius: Full pill (`9999px`) or `10px`
  - Text: `text-secondary`, transitioning to `text-primary` on hover.

### 2. Key Metric Stat Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#161822`, border 1px `#232736`, padding 22px, border-radius 16px.
- **Header**: Flex container with label (`label-caps`, `#64748B`) on the left, accompanied by an interactive circular info icon (`14px`, stroke `#64748B`, hover `#94A3B8`).
- **Data Anchor**: Below the header, 8px spacing, displaying `stat-display` (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Supplementary Context**: Optional secondary trend badge (e.g., small green sparkline or delta pill) positioned right-aligned with the numeric anchor.

### 3. Directional & Categorical Pills
- **Trade Direction (Long / Buy)**: Solid emerald fill (`#10B981`) or soft tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` with `#10B981` text), font `label-pill`, padding 4px 10px, radius `9999px`.
- **Trade Direction (Short / Sell)**: Solid rose fill (`#EF4444`) or soft tint (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)` with `#EF4444` text), font `label-pill`, padding 4px 10px, radius `9999px`.
- **Setup & Session Badges**: Muted surface fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#94A3B8`, radius `9999px`.

### 4. Icon Badges (Trend Indicator)
- **Dimension**: 28px × 28px container, radius 8px.
- **Gain Variant**: Background `gain-emerald-subtle`, containing a centered solid arrow pointing up-right (`#10B981`).
- **Loss Variant**: Background `loss-rose-subtle`, containing a centered solid arrow pointing down-right (`#EF4444`).

### 5. Data Table Rows & Cells
- **Row Architecture**: Min-height 54px. Background transparent in neutral state. Alternating row fills are avoided; clean separation is accomplished via a 1px bottom border (`rgba(35, 39, 54, 0.5)`).
- **Row Hover State**: `#1C1F2E` across all row cells with a smooth 150ms ease transition.
- **Detail Trigger**: Circular icon button (32px diameter, radius 50%) seated at the row end; transparent background, `text-tertiary`, shifting to `#232736` background and `text-primary` on hover.

### 6. Filter Bar
- Inline pill strip with outlined borders (`#232736`). Active filters render as small dismissible pills containing an "×" glyph (`#94A3B8`, hover `#F8FAFC`). Includes an explicit "Clear all" inline link rendered in `text-tertiary` (`13px`).

### 7. Equity Chart Container
- Enclosed inside a primary 16px rounded card. Header features chart title ("Cumulative Equity / R") paired with an inline pill toggle group (e.g., "$ / R-multiple", "1W", "1M", "3M", "YTD", "ALL") styled with soft inset backgrounds.
